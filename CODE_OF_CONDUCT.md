# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience,
or status.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give and receive constructive feedback, and
focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information,
and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are
not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through
GitHub's private contact or security reporting channels. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and handled fairly.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
